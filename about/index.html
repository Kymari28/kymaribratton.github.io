About

I like the part where the mess gets organized.

Most of the work I enjoy starts the same way: an organization has more information or moving parts than people can reasonably hold in their head, and someone needs to make it navigable. A tuition tool, a knowledge graph, a travel concept, a wellness practice: different sectors, same underlying problem.

I'm happiest somewhere new, learning something unexpected, or finding the hidden logic in how people experience the world. That curiosity is what brought me to UX, and why I care about the small moments that make a process feel human.

  • People-first

Capabilities

What I Bring

These overlap more than they separate. Most projects need several at once.

  • Research & insight

    Usability testing, interviews, and synthesis, then translating what I heard into something a team can act on rather than a list of observations.

  • Experience design

    Information architecture, flows, forms, and interfaces. How people find things, understand where they are, and get to what they came for.

  • Project & program coordination

    Keeping work moving: documenting decisions, communicating with clients and stakeholders, and staying on top of what happens next.

  • Systems & process improvement

    Finding the places where an experience is fragmented, repetitive, or confusing, and working out what would actually make it better.

  • Strategy

    Connecting what users need to what the organization needs, and being clear about what should be prioritised when both cannot happen at once.
