Services

How I Can Help

Website design and build

A full site built around how people actually use it — from the first sketch of the strategy through a live, responsive build.

  • Strategy and information architecture
  • UX research and design
  • Responsive design across devices
  • Accessibility built in from the start
  • Full build and launch

UX audit

A close look at a site you already have — where people get confused, where they drop off, and what's standing between a visitor and the action you want them to take.

  • Usability review
  • Accessibility check
  • Navigation and messaging clarity
  • Mobile experience review
  • Clear, prioritized recommendations

Ongoing support

For clients who want a longer relationship — continued updates, improvements, and strategy as your site and your work evolve.

  • Content and design updates
  • Ongoing UX improvements
  • Strategy check-ins
  • Small fixes and larger changes as they come up

Process

How I Work

01

Discovery

We start with a conversation about your goals, your audience, and where things feel stuck. This is where I learn what actually matters to you.

02

Strategy

I turn what I've learned into a clear plan: what the site needs to do, for whom, and in what order.

03

Design

I design the structure, the flow, and the look, and share it with you along the way, not just at the end.

04

Build

I build the site to match, with attention to how it holds up on every device and for every visitor.

05

Handoff

You get a finished site and a clear picture of how it works, so you're not stuck waiting on me for small changes.
