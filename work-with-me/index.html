Process

How I Work

Here's what working together actually looks like, from a first conversation to a finished site.

01

Discovery

A conversation about your goals, your audience, and where things feel stuck, so I know what actually matters before anything gets built.

02

Strategy

I turn what I've learned into a clear plan: what the site needs to do, for whom, and in what order.

03

Design

I design the structure, the flow, and the look, and share it with you along the way, not just at the end.

04

Build

I build the site to match, with attention to how it holds up on every device and for every visitor.

05

Handoff

You get a finished site and a clear picture of how it works, so you're not stuck waiting on me for small changes.
