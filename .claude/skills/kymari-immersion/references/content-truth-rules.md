# Content Truth Rules

Strong positioning is encouraged.

Fabrication is prohibited.

## Claim categories

Every important factual claim should be classified:

VERIFIED

NEEDS CONFIRMATION

UNSUPPORTED

## Never invent

- impact
- launch status
- user counts
- revenue
- conversion
- grant outcomes
- clinical outcomes
- testing sample sizes
- sole ownership
- client results

## Distinguish

MEASURED RESULT

DESIGN INTENT

QUALITATIVE OBSERVATION

PERSONAL CONTRIBUTION

TEAM CONTRIBUTION

Do not combine categories into a stronger unsupported claim.

Example:

Measured:
"Ease-of-use ratings increased from 2.5/5 to 4.5/5 in testing with three participants."

Intent:
"The redesign aimed to make GRC's impact easier for partners and funders to evaluate."

Never transform the second into:
"The redesign increased GRC's funding."

## Ownership

Use strong verbs for work Kymari genuinely owned.

Use collaborative language for team work.

Never imply she independently redesigned an entire system when her ownership was specific.

## Tone

Prefer specific human language.

Avoid unnecessary:

- leveraged
- utilized
- innovative solution
- seamless experience
- revolutionized
- holistic

Example:

Avoid:
"Leveraged data visualization to optimize stakeholder comprehension."

Prefer:
"I reorganized years of impact information so grant writers could understand the bigger picture without digging through a wall of statistics."

## Writing

Natural.
Human.
Confident.
Specific.
Not overly academic.

Do not use em dashes in visible website copy.
