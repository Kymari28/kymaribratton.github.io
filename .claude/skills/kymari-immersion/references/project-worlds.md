# Mindfulness with Desiree

## Feeling

Warm.
Serene.
Safe.
Sunlit.
Human.
Slow.
Beautiful.

Emotional idea:

"You can exhale here."

Possible emotional undercurrent:

"You are safe."
"You are cared for."
"You are loved."

This should primarily be felt rather than stated.

## Environment

Imagine:

sunlight filtering through trees while Desiree performs with sound bowls.

Use:

- authentic warm photography
- forest green
- moss
- cream
- brown
- earth
- muted gold
- natural sunlight
- negative space

Deeper tones may frame the outer composition.

As the visitor moves inward, the experience may become lighter and airier.

## Motion

Do not build the world around obvious ripple effects.

Sound bowls inspire the feeling, not a literal visualizer.

Motion should feel like:

- space opening
- weightlessness
- soft floating
- gentle separation
- release
- settling
- warm light entering

Pointer proximity may move background botanical elements approximately 1 to 3px when appropriate.

Keep it extremely restrained.

No autoplay audio.

Do not fabricate wellness photography.

# Immigrants Rising

## Story

Uncertainty
→ guided journey
→ clarity
→ confidence

## Palette

blue
pale aqua
cream

## Visual language

Use:

- progress
- journey
- pathways
- review
- accessibility
- structure
- questions becoming answers

## Layout storytelling

The environment may become progressively more ordered.

Beginning:
clear but slightly more fragmented visual language.

Middle:
a visible guided path.

End:
structured, aligned, calm, confident.

The beginning must NEVER become intentionally difficult to understand.

## Motion

Predictable.
Restrained.
Trustworthy.

No bouncing.
No unnecessary physics.
