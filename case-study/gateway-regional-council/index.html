How we got there

A team process, before my individual focus.

The wider redesign was a collaborative effort with the Gateway Regional Council team, moving through:

  1. 1Researching comparable nonprofit sites, including New York Cares and Robin Hood, for patterns worth adapting
  2. 2Interviewing people across GRC's key audiences, one persona group per team member
  3. 3Defining user personas and prioritizing the features and content each one actually needed
  4. 4Moving from low-fidelity layouts to high-fidelity Figma designs built for Wix's constraints
  5. 5Implementing in Wix, with custom code where its native components weren't enough
  6. 6Testing with real personas on desktop and mobile, then refining from what we observed

From there, my own focus narrowed to three areas: Impact, Events, and Grant Writers.

Approach

One organization, three audiences.

Community organizations hold a lot of information that matters to very different readers, and a single undifferentiated page asks everyone to dig through all of it to find their piece. The work is about structuring one body of organizational information so it can answer all three without becoming a document dump.

Residents

People looking for programs and resources need to find what applies to them and understand how to access it, without needing to know how the organization is structured internally.

Partners & stakeholders

Partner organizations need a clear picture of what the council does, who it serves, and where collaboration fits.

Funders

Funder-facing readers need organizational data and impact information presented so it can actually be read and interpreted, not just published.

My contribution · Grant Writers

Built for the reader who needs the numbers.

Grant funding is one of GRC's main sources of support, so I focused the Impact page on making that data easy for a grant writer to actually use: scan the key metrics first, then open the year-by-year detail without sorting through a long list of statistics.

  • 10,200 Residents engaged
  • 5,680 Technical assistance hours
  • 196 Partnerships established

All six categories, with year-by-year detail, are explored just below.

What testing revealed. During testing, one participant expected the entire expandable area to be clickable, not just the small dropdown icon. It was a small detail, but it changed how I think about interaction targets.

Custom interaction · Events

Too many events to just list.

Wix's page builder handles layout, not filtering. I designed a custom Grid/Calendar view with search and focus-area filtering built directly into the page, so residents can narrow a busy month down to what actually applies to them.

  1. 1 Grid/Calendar toggle, so residents can scan a month or a list
  2. 2 Keyword search across every event
  3. 3 Focus-area filter, narrowing by program type
  4. 4 Month navigation with a one-tap return to today
  5. 5 Selected-day detail panel with time, location, and a direct link
